Lab Milestones

2026

Vice Chair & Division Director

Dr. Stanic appointed Vice Chair for Basic and Translational Research and Director of the Division of Reproductive Sciences.

2025

Multiple Conference Awards

Logan Keding wins ASRI Poster Award and IFPA Oral Presentation Award. Lab presents at SRI, ASRI, and IFPA.

2024

Jessica Vazquez Awarded NIH K01

First K01 from the lab — supporting an independent program on maternal immune programming in rhesus macaques.

2024

Spatial Transcriptomics Atlas Launched

Cross-gestational Visium FFPE atlas of the rhesus macaque maternal-fetal interface — the first of its kind.

2022

Tenure Awarded

Dr. Stanic receives tenure at UW-Madison, recognizing contributions to reproductive immunology.
