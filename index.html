Our Mission

Bridging Clinic and Discovery

We are a physician-scientist-led research group investigating how the immune system functions across the full reproductive continuum — from ovarian biology and fertility, through the maternal-fetal interface during pregnancy, to postpartum recovery. Rooted in fundamental innate immunology, our work integrates clinical REI practice, multi-omic single-cell technologies, spatial transcriptomics, and computational biology to understand immune regulation in reproduction.
